Before Your Session

Look ready. Feel ready.

A short guide to wardrobe, timing, and what to expect, sent after booking so there are no surprises on session day.

What to wear

Do

  • Solid colors and jewel tones (navy, burgundy, forest green, charcoal)
  • Well-fitted clothing, especially through the shoulders and collar
  • Bring 2-3 outfit options if your package includes multiple looks
  • Subtle, simple jewelry
  • Matte skin: a light powder helps cut shine under studio light

Avoid

  • All-white or all-black (both are hard to light well)
  • Busy patterns, stripes, or logos
  • Clothing that closely matches the background color
  • Anything brand-new and unworn: comfort shows on camera
  • Heavy, glossy makeup
Session-day timeline
Day before
Lay out your outfit(s) and confirm the session time and location. Charge your phone if you're using it for reference shots.
Morning of
Arrive a few minutes early. If hair/makeup isn't included, plan to finish that beforehand. Natural, camera-ready is the goal, not full glam.
During
Expect direction on posing throughout: chin position, shoulder angle, weight distribution. A few different expressions will be captured: approachable, confident, and a natural candid.
After
Proofs are typically ready within a few business days, delivered through a private online gallery.
