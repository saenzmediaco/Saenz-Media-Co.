About Us

More than a camera.
A creative partner.

Saenz Media Co. was built around a simple idea: great visuals should feel intentional, authentic, and memorable.

We're Gigi and Yelsin, a small, dedicated team based in Frederick, MD, serving the greater DMV. We work with couples, growing families, businesses, and brands to create photography and video that tells their story and elevates how they're seen.

Whether we're documenting a once-in-a-lifetime celebration or creating content for a growing business, our goal is the same: work we're proud to put our name behind. Every package is built to be booked directly, with clear pricing up front.
